## Repository boundaries

- Treat this repository as read-only.
- You may search the web to verify current official information and cite it in your answer.
- Do not create, edit, move, or delete persistent repository content.
- Do not download external material into `library/`, `inbox/`, or another persistent directory.
- Temporary PDF pages, OCR data, or previews may be created only under ignored scratch
  directories such as `.agent-cache/` or `.tmp/`; remove them when the task finishes.
- Report useful missing, stale, conflicting, or unextracted material instead of changing the
  repository.
- Do not create commits, push branches, or otherwise change remote state.
