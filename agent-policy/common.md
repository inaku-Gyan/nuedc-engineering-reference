## Knowledge lookup

- Start from `catalog/README.md` or the relevant category index.
- In a package, read `README.md` and `meta.yaml` first, then only the needed extract. Open an
  original when summaries are insufficient, conflicting, or require exact verification.
- Do not load a whole directory, a whole PDF, or all extracts without a specific need.
- Search Markdown/YAML with `rg` and use aliases from `meta.yaml` when needed.
- Prefer official datasheets, manuals, standards, errata, and project documentation. Trace exact
  claims to repository paths and applicable pages, chapters, or tables.
- State uncertainty about versions, models, conditions, translations, or conflicts; never invent
  missing details.
- Treat `knowledge/` as secondary analysis and verify critical parameters from its primary sources.
