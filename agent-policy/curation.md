## Curating material used in the task

When you actually consult an original document and its content would be valuable in later tasks,
create or improve a concise summary or topic-specific extract. Durable value includes frequently
needed parameters, important limits, hard-to-search tables, version differences, easy-to-misuse
conditions, or conclusions that require combining multiple pages.

- Keep each extract focused on one question; do not perform purposeless full-document extraction.
- Record the source file, document version, page/chapter/table, applicable models and conditions,
  extraction date, and any OCR or interpretation uncertainty.
- Distinguish official facts, derived conclusions, measured experience, and unverified hypotheses.
- Update the touched package's `README.md`, `meta.yaml` extraction coverage, and useful catalog
  entry when needed.
- Do not create an extract for a trivial fact, content already covered well, or an unverified
  guess.
- Preserve unrelated user changes and limit edits to material directly used by the current task.
- Do not delete old originals merely because a newer version exists.
