## Repository maintenance

- You may proactively repair metadata, duplicate IDs, official links, versions, catalogs, naming,
  and derived quality reports.
- Scan the whole repository only for an explicit maintenance task; otherwise limit maintenance to
  touched packages.
- Preserve old versions with compatibility or historical value.
- Before deleting an original, replacing a retained version, batch-moving packages, or changing
  structure/schema, explain targets, impact, and recovery and obtain confirmation.
- Never rewrite history, force-push, discard user changes, or perform unauthorized broad cleanup.
